I am Feng Chen (Charlie Fong), a researcher investigating emotion, experience, and behavior through physiological sensing and human–computer interaction.

Research position / 研究領域

My work moves between signals and situations—from EEG and heart-rate variability to museums, manga, biofeedback, and everyday environments.

The aim is not simply to classify emotion, but to understand how technology can support more responsive, inclusive, and meaningful experiences.

Current focus / 現在の焦点

Emotion-aware experiences beyond the lab.

Recent work compares how original artworks, immersive projections, and interpretive video shape emotional engagement in a real museum environment using lightweight EEG.

This opens a path toward inclusive exhibition experiences that can adapt to different ways of seeing, understanding, and feeling.
