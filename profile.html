Biography

Research across signals, systems, and lived experience.

Feng Chen is a researcher at the SIT Research Laboratories, Shibaura Institute of Technology. The work investigates how physiological signals—including EEG and heart-rate variability—can reveal emotion, attention, stress, arousal, and engagement.

Since 2016, this research has expanded from bio-emotion estimation and communication systems to experience-centered studies involving robots, social media, aroma, fashion, packaging, manga, food, learning environments, and museums. The central interest is translating measurable evidence about internal states into more responsive and inclusive technologies, media, and environments.
